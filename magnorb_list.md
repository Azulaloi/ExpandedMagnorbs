Internal names for all Magnorb bracers in Expanded Magnorbs 2.1, and how to make them

## Main
> Makeshift Magnorbs - makemagnorbs
Crafted from Iron, at the Lodestone Stand.

> Sturdy Magnorbs - sturdymagnorbs
Crafted from Tungsten, at the Lodestone Stand.

> Shiny Magnorbs - shinymagnorbs
Crafted from Titanium, at the Magnetic Induction Station.

> Durable Magnorbs - duramagnorbs
Crafted from Durasteel, at the Magnetic Induction Station.

## Elementals
> Chilled Magnorbs - chillmagnorbs
Crafted from Cryonic Extract and Shiny Magnorbs, at the Magnetic Induction Station.

> Electrified Magnorbs - elecmagnorbs
Crafted from Static Cells and Shiny Magnorbs, at the Magnetic Induction Station.

> Heated Magnorbs - heatmagnorbs
Crafted from Scorched Cores and Shiny Magnorbs, at the Magnetic Induction Station.

> Poisoned Magnorbs - poismagnorbs
Crafted from Venom Samples and Shiny Magnorbs, at the Magnetic Induction Station.
 
## Upgraded
> Long Range Magnorbs - aemagnorbs
Crafted from Refined Aegisalt, at the Gauss Manipulator.

> High-Velocity Magnorbs - aesmagnorbs
Crafted from Long Range Magnorbs and Solarium Stars, at the Gauss Manipulator.

> Shredding Magnorbs - vimagnorbs
Crafted from Refined Violium, at the Gauss Manipulator.

> High-Speed Magnorbs - vismagnorbs
Crafted from Shredding Magnorbs and Solarium Stars, at the Gauss Manipulator.

> Multiple Magnorbs - femagnorbs
Crafted from Refined Ferozium, at the Gauss Manipulator.

> High-Count Magnorbs - fesmagnorbs
Crafted from Multiple Magnorbs and Solarium Stars, at the Gauss Manipulator.
 
> Solarium Magnorbs - somagnorbs
Crafted from Refined Aegisalt, Refined Violium, Refined Ferozium, and Solarium Stars, at the Gauss Manipulator.

## Special
> Unstable Magnorbs - unstablemagnorbs
Crafted from Core Fragments and Durable Magnorbs, at the Magnetic Induction Station. Recipe learned on acquiring Core Fragments.

> Phasic Magnorbs - phantommagnorbs
Crafted from Phase Matter and Durable Magnorbs, at the Magnetic Induction Station. Recipe learned on acquiring Phase Matter.

> Asuteru Obu - rainbowmagnorbs
Crafted from Prisilite and Durable Magnorbs, at the Magnetic Induction Station. Recipe learned on acquiring the Asuteru Saberu DX. May also be found in rainbow biome treasure chests (/rainbowTreasure/0/1/).
 
> Amplified Magnorbs - ampmagnorbs
Crafted from the vanilla Magnorbs, at the Magnetic Induction Station. Recipe learned on acquiring vanilla Magnorbs.
 
## Objects
> Lodestone Stands - craftingmagnorbs
Crafting station for all craftable bracers. Crafted at the Inventor's Table.

Upgrades through three tiers: Lodestone Stand (T1), Magnetic Induction Station (T2), and Gauss Manipulator (T3)

## Items
> Lodestone
Crafting ingredient, made at the Lodestone Stand.
